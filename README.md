# 🌱 Cambium — AI-Powered Research Opportunity Discovery & Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

Cambium is an AI-powered Research Opportunity Discovery and Intelligence Platform designed to help researchers discover grants, Calls for Papers (CFPs), journals, research papers, collaborators, and emerging research trends from a single platform.

Researchers currently spend significant time searching across multiple websites, institutional portals, mailing lists, and databases for relevant opportunities. Cambium aims to simplify this process by aggregating trusted research resources into one intelligent workspace and providing personalized AI-powered recommendations based on each researcher's interests.

Instead of replacing researchers, Cambium acts as an intelligent research assistant that reduces discovery time, improves productivity, and supports informed research decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Research opportunities are scattered across multiple platforms, making discovery inefficient and time-consuming. Researchers often miss valuable funding opportunities, suitable publication venues, and emerging research trends due to fragmented workflows.

Cambium addresses this challenge by providing a centralized platform that intelligently recommends relevant opportunities based on user interests and research profiles.
