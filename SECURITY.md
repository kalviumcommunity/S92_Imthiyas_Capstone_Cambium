## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not disclose security vulnerabilities publicly through issues or discussions.

1. Email detailed reproduction steps and logs to:
   - **Lead Architect**: Shaik Mohamed Imthiyas T (`shaik.imthiyas.t@gmail.com`)
   - **Security Team**: `security@cambium.research`
2. You will receive an acknowledgment within 24 hours.
3. Fixes are deployed via emergency hotfix branches with zero regression verification.
