### Product Scope
Cambium operates across 4 core scholarly pillars:
- **Pillar I**: Discovery, Knowledge Topology & AI Intelligence (120M+ indexed publications, force-directed citation graphs).
- **Pillar II**: Execution, Operations & Lab Compute (lab computing allocations, shared project workspaces).
- **Pillar III**: Funding, Ethics, Governance & Security (grant RFP matching, IRB protocols, cryptographic audit ledger).
- **Pillar IV**: Dissemination, Peer Review & Verified Identity (ORCID synchronization, COPE ethical peer review, BibTeX/APA citation generation).
