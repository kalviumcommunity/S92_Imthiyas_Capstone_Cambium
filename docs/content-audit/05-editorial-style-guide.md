# Cambium Content Audit — Document 05: Editorial Style Guide

---

## 1. Voice and Tone Matrix

| Context | Target Voice | Example | Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marketing & Landing** | Composed, intelligent, clear | *"Find research opportunities, trace citation connections, and organize collaborative workspaces."* | *"Unlock endless possibilities with the world's most revolutionary research platform."* |
| **Product Interface** | Direct, functional, calm | *"Upload Manuscript (PDF)"*, *"Filter by funding agency"* | *"Engage Document Ingestion Core"*, *"Empower Search"* |
| **Empty States** | Helpful, contextual, concise | *"No saved publications. Save papers from Discover to read them later."* | *"Your knowledge garden is empty. Start your incredible journey today!"* |
| **Error Handling** | Reassuring, actionable | *"We couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again."* | *"System Fault 0x48: Neural pipeline severed."* |
| **Success Messages** | Understated, fast | *"Note saved."*, *"Workspace created."* | *"Congratulations! You have reached a new milestone in your productivity journey!"* |

---

## 2. Punctuation and Formatting Standards
1. **Sentence Case**: Use sentence case for button labels (*"Create workspace"* not *"CREATE WORKSPACE"*), tabs, card headers, and navigation links.
2. **Em Dash Restraint**: Reserve em dashes for genuine parenthetical breaks in longer prose. Never use them as decorative separators between two clauses.
3. **Exclamation Marks**: Prohibited in standard product navigation, system notices, and confirmation dialogs.
4. **Numbers**: Use digits for countable metrics (*"14 days left"*, *"5 collaborators"*). Write out small numbers in prose (*"three primary fields"*).
