# Cambium Research — India DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025 Regulatory Readiness Matrix

> **Critical Legal Notice**: As of **27 November 2026**, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (notified November 2025 under Gazette notifications including G.S.R. 843(E)) operate on a **phased commencement schedule**: one-year provisions (including Rule 4) commenced on **13 November 2026**, while principal operational obligations of the Act and Rules 3, 5–16, 22, and 23 are scheduled for commencement after 18 months on **13 May 2027**, subject to any subsequent Gazette corrigenda or notifications.
> **Non-Certification Warning**: Technical readiness controls listed below do **not** constitute legal compliance certification. Legal applicability (Data Fiduciary vs Processor status, Significant Data Fiduciary thresholds, Institutional exemptions) requires qualified legal review.
