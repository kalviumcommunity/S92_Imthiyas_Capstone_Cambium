RESEARCH DISCOVERY ENGINE

Act as a Principal Product Designer and UX Architect designing the flagship
Discover experience for Cambium, a premium Research Operating System.

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

Cambium is NOT:

- a generic search engine
- Google Scholar
- a social media feed
- a job board
- a grant database
- a generic AI SaaS dashboard
- a paper directory

Cambium's Discover experience connects:

- Research papers
- Researchers
- Research topics
- Projects
- Labs
- Datasets
- Journals
- Conferences
- Emerging research trends
- Research opportunities
- Related ideas

The goal is to transform research discovery from:

"search → open result → repeat"

into:

"discover → understand → connect → explore → act"

Use these products only as quality references:

Linear
Notion
GitHub
Perplexity
Semantic Scholar
ResearchRabbit
Google Scholar
Apple

DO NOT copy their interfaces.

Create an original Cambium experience.

==================================================
CORE QUESTION
==================================================

The Discover page must answer:

"What research should I explore next?"

"I didn't know this research existed."

"I understand why it matters."

"I want to explore this."

Discover should feel like entering a living research ecosystem.
