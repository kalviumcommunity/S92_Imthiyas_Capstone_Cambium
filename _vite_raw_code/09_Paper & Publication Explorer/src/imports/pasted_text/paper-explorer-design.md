CAMBIUM — PAGE 07
PAPER & PUBLICATION EXPLORER
FLAGSHIP ACADEMIC LITERATURE EXPERIENCE

Design a premium, production-quality desktop web application interface
for CAMBIUM — the world's first Research Operating System.

This is PAGE 07: PAPER & PUBLICATION EXPLORER.

Do NOT design a generic academic search engine.

Do NOT design a Google Scholar clone.

Do NOT design a generic document library.

Do NOT design a social feed.

This experience should become Cambium's primary environment for
discovering, understanding, organizing and acting on academic
literature and publication venues.

============================================================
1. CORE QUESTION
============================================================

The page must answer:

"What research should I read, understand, save, cite, and potentially
publish?"

The user should be able to move naturally between:

DISCOVER RESEARCH
        ↓
UNDERSTAND PAPER
        ↓
EXPLORE RELATED WORK
        ↓
SAVE / CITE
        ↓
ADD TO WORKSPACE
        ↓
FIND PUBLICATION VENUE
        ↓
PLAN PUBLICATION

The experience must feel like part of a Research OS rather than a
separate academic database.
