# CAMBIUM — RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE
## PAGE 11 — AI RESEARCH ASSISTANT + RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE

Act as a Principal Product Designer and UX Architect at the level of Linear, Notion, GitHub, Apple, Figma and Perplexity.

Design Page 11 of Cambium:

🤖 RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

Research Intelligence is Cambium's intelligence layer.

It helps researchers:

- understand research
- discover relevant knowledge
- connect papers
- identify research gaps
- explore research questions
- analyze literature
- improve methodology
- find opportunities
- discover collaborators
- synthesize information
- navigate their research world

This is NOT:

- a generic AI chatbot
- ChatGPT clone
- a simple search box
- a generic AI SaaS assistant
- an AI writing toy
- a prompt playground

The core idea is:

> "Cambium understands my research context."

The intelligence layer should connect:

Researcher
→ Workspace
→ Papers
→ Notes
→ Projects
→ Publications
→ Opportunities
→ Researchers
→ Topics
→ Questions

into one intelligent research environment.

8. RESEARCH ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------

When a query is submitted, display a polished research response.

Example query:

"What are the main research gaps in low-resource medical image segmentation?"

Response:

"Based on 18 papers in your current literature review, three recurring gaps stand out."

Then show:

01

Limited cross-domain validation

Several studies evaluate models on a single dataset, making generalization difficult to establish.

Sources:

Paper A

Paper B

Paper C

02

Limited evaluation under label scarcity

Most approaches assume more labeled data than is available in low-resource clinical environments.

Sources:

Paper D

Paper E

03

Privacy and distributed learning remain underexplored

Federated approaches appear promising, but evaluation across heterogeneous clinical datasets remains limited.

Sources:

Paper F

Paper G

The answer must visually prioritize:

Claim

Evidence

Sources

Confidence/context

Do not make the response look like an ordinary chat bubble.
