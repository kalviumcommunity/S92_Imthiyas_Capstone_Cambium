Design Page 06 of Cambium:

RESEARCH WORKSPACE

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

The Research Workspace is one of Cambium's signature experiences.

It is NOT a simple notes application.

It is NOT a generic document editor.

It is NOT a project management dashboard.

It is the researcher's digital laboratory.

This is where a researcher:

• thinks
• writes
• organizes knowledge
• reads papers
• connects ideas
• manages references
• creates research questions
• plans experiments
• records observations
• analyzes results
• collaborates
• develops papers
• moves research toward publication

If Academic Identity represents:

"WHO AM I?"

Then Home represents:

"WHAT IS HAPPENING?"

Research Workspace represents:

"WHAT AM I BUILDING?"

The final emotional response should be:

"This is the best place I've ever worked on research."

Transformer Models for Medical Imaging

Research Question:

How can transformer-based segmentation models maintain performance when labeled medical imaging datasets are limited?

Research Gap:

Existing medical imaging foundation models often depend on large annotated datasets that are difficult to obtain in low-resource clinical environments.

Hypothesis:

Lightweight transformer architectures combined with self-supervised pretraining can achieve competitive segmentation performance with significantly less labeled training data.

Research Objective:

Evaluate lightweight transformer architectures under low-data conditions.
