# CAMBIUM — COLLABORATION HUB

Act as a Principal Product Designer and UX Architect at the level of Linear, Notion, GitHub, Apple, Figma and Perplexity.

Design Page 9 of Cambium:

🤝 COLLABORATION HUB

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

The Collaboration Hub is where researchers discover the right people to work with, understand their expertise, find research communities, start conversations, and form meaningful academic collaborations.

This is NOT:

- LinkedIn
- Twitter/X
- a generic social network
- a freelancer marketplace
- a dating-style matching system
- a generic people directory
- a job board
- a messaging application

It should feel like a professional research network built around:

Research interests
Expertise
Projects
Papers
Methods
Datasets
Labs
Institutions
Research questions
Collaboration needs

The central idea is:

"Who should I be connected with to move my research forward?"

--------------------------------------------------
1. CORE USER QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------

The Collaboration Hub must answer:

"Who could help me move my research forward?"

The user should be able to discover:

- researchers
- collaborators
- research labs
- research groups
- mentors
- domain experts
- interdisciplinary researchers
- potential co-authors
- dataset contributors
- methodology experts
- project partners

Every recommendation should explain WHY the person or lab is relevant.

Examples:

"Strong match · Computer Vision"

"Works on medical imaging"

"Connected to 3 papers you're reading"

"Looking for collaborators in federated learning"

"Shared interest: Low-resource AI"

Never recommend people without contextual reasoning.

- Use meaningless animations

--------------------------------------------------

The Collaboration Hub should make Cambium feel fundamentally different from conventional academic platforms.

The researcher should think:

"I don't just discover papers here."

"I discover the people who can help me answer better questions."

"I can see where my research overlaps with others."

"I can understand why someone is relevant to my work."

"I can find collaborators without searching blindly."

The final emotional response should be:

> "Cambium helps my research connect to the right people."

Design the Collaboration Hub as a world-class, premium Research OS experience.
