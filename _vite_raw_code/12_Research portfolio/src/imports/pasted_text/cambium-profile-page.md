# CAMBIUM — RESEARCH PORTFOLIO / PROFILE
## PAGE 10 — PUBLIC RESEARCH IDENTITY

Act as a Principal Product Designer and UX Architect at the level of Linear, Notion, GitHub, Apple, Figma and Perplexity.

Design Page 10 of Cambium:

🗂️ RESEARCH PORTFOLIO / PROFILE

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

This page is the researcher's public academic identity and living research portfolio.

The Academic Identity page answers:

"Who am I as a researcher?"

This page answers:

"What have I actually done, what am I working on, and what does my research world look like?"

The profile should feel like a combination of:

Academic identity
Research portfolio
Publications
Projects
Research interests
Research activity
Knowledge
Collaborations
Academic presence

It is NOT a LinkedIn profile.

It is NOT a resume.

It is NOT a social-media profile.

It is NOT a generic portfolio website.

It is a living research identity powered by everything the researcher does inside Cambium.

Restrained botanical / forest green

Inter or Geist.

Profile name:

28–36px

Section heading:

20–24px

Card title:

16–18px

Body:

14–16px

Metadata:

12–13px

Use an 8px spacing system.

Primary rhythm:

16 / 24 / 32 / 48px.

Cards:

Minimal

Flat

Subtle 1px borders

Very restrained shadows

Moderate radius
