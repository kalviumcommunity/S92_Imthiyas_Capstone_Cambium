PERSONAL RESEARCH OS

Cambium is the world's first Research Operating System.

This page is the personal control layer that helps a researcher organize
their time, saved knowledge, notifications, research routines and
preferences across the entire Cambium ecosystem.

This is NOT a generic settings page.

This is NOT an analytics dashboard.

This is NOT another workspace.

It should feel like the researcher's personal command center for
organizing their research life.

The experience must be calm, intelligent, highly organized and premium.
