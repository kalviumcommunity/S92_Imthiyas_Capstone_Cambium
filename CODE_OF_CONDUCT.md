# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Cambium Research a harassment-free scholarly and engineering experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected Standards

- Constructive, evidence-based technical and academic feedback.
- Respect for confidential research artifacts, institutional privacy, and ethical peer review norms (COPE guidelines).
- Zero harassment, derogatory remarks, or unauthorized disclosure of private collaborator details.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository lead maintainer at `shaik.imthiyas.t@gmail.com`. Reports are reviewed confidentially in line with repository governance.
